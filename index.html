enki.ngo · a public-good, non-profit association

Intelligence belongs on your devices, not in their datacenters.

A public-good association of 300 — no fee, one vote each. We hold to a manifesto, draw open blueprints for a pocket-sized local AI datacenter anyone can mass-produce, and write the standard that meshes your devices into one. Nothing is “free” here: the registry publishes what open models really cost.
