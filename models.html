04 · The registry

Open weights.
Honest costs.

Open models we actually trust — each one runnable on hardware you own: a desktop GPU, a laptop, even a phone. For each: what it’s good at, where it falls short, what the datacenter APIs charge, and what it really costs to run yourself. Members vote models in and out as the frontier moves.

Browse the modelsPropose a model

Nothing here is “free” — open weights still cost compute. Hosted is the datacenter-API reference rate per 1M tokens (in / out); self-host is our estimated cost on your own hardware. Both move — the registry keeps the receipts.
