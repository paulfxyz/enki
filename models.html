Cost assumptions

All costs and ratios here are estimates, not audited results. We do not yet have enough measured data to claim certainty. Where a hosted price is missing, we use an indicative peer estimate, not a provider quote. We hope to build a better, measured Registry once the founding 300 are assembled.

USD per million total tokens, with 80% input and 20% output. Self-hosting assumes purchased hardware amortised over three years, 50% utilisation, electricity at $0.20/kWh and illustrative batched throughput. It excludes labour, networking, tax and redundancy. Memory fit, latency, quality and throughput are not validated; the figures are scenarios, not Enki device benchmarks. Tap a comparison to see its assumptions.
