01 · The Institute

Research on what AI does to people — not just what it can do.

02 · The Advisory

Consultancy for the rooms where policy is written and capital moves.

A consultancy for parties, governments, institutions and investors — one mandate: put intelligence on people’s devices, not in a handful of datacenters. Established in force once the standard stands.

For policy rooms

Parties, governments & public institutions

  • Local-first AI policy frameworks — intelligence on citizens’ devices, not rented from sovereign clouds
  • The international device standard — every GPU/NPU device model-updated, mesh-enrolled, gateway-recognised
  • National sovereignty strategies without hyperscaler dependency
  • KYC/KYB compliance models for licensed high-compute AI
  • Three-tier governance — frontier AI gated like bio-containment labs, research compute licensed, personal AI free and local
  • Public procurement playbooks favouring open weights and local inference

For capital

Funds, family offices & industrial investors

  • Semiconductor and edge-inference theses beyond datacenter GPUs
  • Due diligence against the falling cost floor — what $725B of datacenter capex misses
  • Market maps of the open-model economy, drawn straight from the registries
Why the split

Split intelligence in two — and society gets a dial.

Keeping the frontier in declared datacenter silos makes superintelligence's reach into society a policy decision, not a market accident: its access is offered progressively and under heavy regulation, widened as oversight matures — and narrowed the day it fails. Meanwhile everyday intelligence — decentralised, local, effectively free — deploys everywhere around us and keeps getting better, carrying 99% of what humans and organisations actually need. The Institute measures both sides of that dial; the Advisory carries the evidence into the rooms that set it.

Superintelligence — siloed and metered

Confined to known, declared datacenters under international rules. Opened progressively — by licence, by sector, by audit — to selected customers under serious compliance, and unpluggable in an emergency without taking daily life down with it.

Controlled rollout

Everyday intelligence — everywhere, local

Small open models on your own hardware and the local mesh — in homes, schools, clinics and companies, effectively free and improving every year. The 99% of what people and organisations actually ask of a machine.
