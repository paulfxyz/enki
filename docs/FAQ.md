# FAQ

These answers describe the English proposal as of 11 October 2026. They distinguish current status from the founding rules and products still to be built.

## Is Enki a company or registered association?

Enki is an unregistered founding initiative with a nonprofit, public-good purpose. Founding members would select its legal form and jurisdiction with qualified counsel; the website does not confer legal or charitable status.

## Does membership cost anything?

There is no membership fee. Selection would consider contributions of skills, networks or mission-aligned resources, but funding cannot purchase admission or special voting rights.

## Why 300 members?

The founding target is 299 individuals plus Paul Fleury, not a claim that all seats are filled. The proposal gives each member one vote, rotates thirty seats annually at full membership, and provides no permanently reserved founder's seat; the procedures still need ratification.

## Can a company join?

The proposal allows individual members, not corporate or proxy seats. Identity checks and interviews are intended before admission, but the provider, data handling and process must be agreed before identity documents are collected.

## Is the device available?

No prototype, validated bill of materials or production-ready blueprint is claimed. The proposed device, mesh, client integrations and Context Vault are design objectives rather than shipped products.

## Is Enki against large AI models?

No. It argues for independent evaluation and proportionate oversight of consequential capabilities, alongside local-first options for suitable everyday tasks. Smaller models still need safeguards; isolation does not guarantee that a powerful system cannot cause harm.

## Is local AI free or always private?

No. Hardware, electricity, maintenance and support cost money, while hosted inference has provider charges. Local-only processing can reduce disclosure to cloud providers, but malware, unsafe tools, cloud-connected clients and compromised devices remain risks.

## How do I apply?

Use the application linked from the website. Submission is intended for human review, not automatic admission, and no reply date is guaranteed. Do not send identity documents or sensitive third-party information in the initial application; retain the page if an error says receipt could not be confirmed.
