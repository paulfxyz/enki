Privacy · English notice

Your application.
Your information.

Enki is a founding initiative, not yet a registered organisation. This notice explains how its website forms work and who is responsible for the information you send.

Updated 11 October 2026 · English only

Who is responsible

I am Paul Fleury, based in Lisbon, Portugal, and the interim data controller for Enki's applications and enquiries. Contact me at hello@enki.ngo for questions, to withdraw an application or to exercise your data rights.

What you send, and why

The application asks for your name, email, country of residence, chosen contribution lane, skills, a professional link and an example of your work. You can provide the example as text, a file or a voice recording; recording is optional, and the browser asks for microphone permission. Please do not send identity documents, sensitive personal information or confidential information about other people.

I use this information to consider your application and communicate with you about it. Contact forms collect your name, email, optional organisation, enquiry category and message so I can respond. Submitting is voluntary, but without the required fields I cannot meaningfully assess an application or reply to an enquiry.

The legal basis is legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f): managing the founding initiative's voluntary applications and enquiries, and protecting its forms against abuse. These purposes are limited to what you reasonably expect when contacting Enki; they are not permission for unrelated marketing, sale of your information or AI training. The European Commission explains this legal basis and its limits.

Access and hosting

Applications are private, not entries on a public membership list. Access is restricted to me and, only where needed, people I authorise to help review or administer them under confidentiality obligations. This form does not make automated admission decisions, rank applicants with AI or send their submissions to an AI model.

The forms send their contents over HTTPS to enki.ngo, where SiteGround hosts the submissions database outside the public website directory. The configured primary data centre is Eemshaven, Netherlands. SiteGround and its infrastructure/support providers process data to operate that service; email providers also handle correspondence when we write to each other. EU primary hosting is not a promise that every support operation or provider processes data only in the EU.

The enki.pplx.app mirror is hosted by Perplexity; its forms still submit directly to enki.ngo. Google serves this site's fonts, so loading a page sends ordinary request information, including an IP address, to Google. Hosting providers also receive request metadata. These services are distinct from the application database; ask me for the applicable processor and international-transfer safeguards before sending information if you need further detail.

Retention, logs and browser storage

Application records, including attachments and recordings, have a 12-month retention limit from submission. Expired applications are removed from the active database by hourly cleanup and before an administrative export. Earlier deletion requests are considered under your applicable rights. Any future membership record would require a separate notice; admission does not silently extend the lifetime of the original application.

The application database records a submission time, form/page identifier, browser user agent and a salted IP hash for abuse prevention. The hash is pseudonymous, not anonymous. The hash and user-agent field are cleared after 24 hours during cleanup; the host's separate access logs can contain the full IP address and requested URL. SiteGround documents access-log archives of up to 30 days.

Deleting a live record does not instantly remove older recovery copies. SiteGround's standard shared-hosting service keeps 30 daily backups; those copies roll off separately and are not used for routine applicant review. Deletions must be reapplied if a backup is restored. Enquiry correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the enquiry and any specific legal obligation or dispute, then reviewed for deletion; it is not covered by the application's automatic expiry.

The site stores your chosen language, theme, accessibility preferences and film-dismissal preference in your browser. It does not save an application draft in browser storage: unsent answers, attachments and recordings stay in the current page's memory and may be lost when you reload or close it. The site code does not add advertising or behavioural-analytics scripts.

Your rights and later identity checks

You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Portability applies where its legal conditions are met. Write to hello@enki.ngo; I may ask for proportionate information to verify that a request is yours, but please do not attach an identity document unsolicited. You can also complain to Portugal's CNPD or your competent data-protection authority.

No identity-verification provider or document-checking process has been selected for membership. Before introducing one, I will explain the information required, the reason and legal basis, who receives it, where it is processed, how long it is kept and any available alternative. Any change of controller when Enki is registered will also be communicated before applications are transferred.
